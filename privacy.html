Casting Digest privacy policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

What it accesses

Casting Digest asks Google for one permission: gmail.send, which lets it send email from its owner's Gmail account. It cannot read, search, change or delete any email, contacts or other Google data.

How it is used

The permission is used only to send casting digest emails from the owner's account to the owner and to the people the owner has chosen to receive them.

What it collects

Casting Digest does not collect, sell or share personal data from anyone. It has no public users and no sign-ups. Google user data is not transferred to third parties or used for advertising.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact
